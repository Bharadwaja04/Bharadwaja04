## 🔭 What I'm building right now

> **📱 An AI incident-report assistant that runs 100% offline on an Android phone**
> A local Qwen3-1.7B model, Whisper speech-to-text and Piper text-to-speech all running on a Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 board. You speak, it listens, asks follow-up questions and files a structured report. No cloud, no internet.

> **🛰️ Satellite-image ML**
> Flood segmentation with a U-Net for AISEHack 2026, plus 10 m → 5 m super-resolution for Sentinel-2 imagery.

> **💸 FinSaarthi**
> A cashflow copilot for small businesses: bank SMS in, categorised spending, dashboards and a 7-day forecast out. [It's live.](https://fin-saarthi.vercel.app)
